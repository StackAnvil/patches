Bedrock reports a mount's saddle with the `SADDLED` actor flag and a tamed horse with `TAMED`. Java keeps the saddle in the `SADDLE` equipment slot and the tame state in the horse flags byte, and neither was translated. A saddled horse therefore looked unsaddled to Java: no saddle model and no jump bar. This sends a `SADDLE` equipment update when the flag changes for saddleable mounts (not llamas) and the tame, eating and standing horse bits. Once Java sees a jumpable mount it sends `START_RIDING_JUMP` and `STOP_RIDING_JUMP`, which are now accepted without a Bedrock packet.

Validation: a Bedrock 1.26.60.5 client riding a tamed, saddled horse on a local BDS shows the jump bar in place of the experience bar and sends only the held jump input flags for a charged jump (`JumpPressedRaw`, `JumpDown`, `Jumping`, `WantUp`, then `JumpReleasedRaw`). On the add-on client the same horse now shows its saddle and jump bar, and three charged jumps send that sequence without disconnecting. Unit tests cover the horse flag bits and which mounts take a saddle.
